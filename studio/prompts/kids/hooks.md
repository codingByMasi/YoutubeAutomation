Hook library for kids' stories (first 8 seconds). A kids hook says hello, names the character and shows the goal.
Short words, one idea, a question the child can answer out loud.

1. cold_open: "Look! It is Pip the duck. Pip wants to find the red ball!"
2. hidden_answer: "Where did the red ball go? Can you see it?"
3. challenge_guess: "How many ducks can you count? Let's count together!"

Hook rules: first sentence 8 words or fewer; a friendly greeting is fine; always warm; never scary; no brands; no
links; no asks for personal information.

Devices: repetition ("one little duck, two little ducks"), call-and-response ("Can you say quack? Quack!"),
counting moments, a tiny obstacle that resolves happily, a goodbye wave.
