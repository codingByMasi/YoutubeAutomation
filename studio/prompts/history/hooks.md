Hook formula library for history and story videos (first 15-20 seconds). A hook drops the viewer into a moment,
opens a question they cannot answer yet, and promises the story will answer it. No greeting, no "today we".

1. cold_open: second person, one concrete sense detail, one decision.
   "You are standing on the dock at dawn. The harbour is too quiet. Somewhere behind you, a bell stops ringing."
2. hidden_answer: ask what should not be possible.
   "How does a city of two hundred thousand people vanish in a single winter?"
3. contradiction: the accepted story is wrong or incomplete.
   "Every textbook says the siege failed. The siege never ended."
4. before_after: the same place, two dates.
   "In March it was the richest street in Europe. By June, nobody would walk down it."
5. challenge_guess: invite a guess, reveal later.
   "Guess who gave the order. Hold that name, because you will be wrong."
6. villain_system: a quiet mechanism, not a cartoon villain.
   "No one planned the famine. It was a spreadsheet, a deadline and a signature."

Hook rules: first sentence 16 words or fewer; second person is allowed, claims about real people stay factual;
promise a payoff ("and by the end you will know who opened the gate"); never graphic detail in the first line.

Devices that keep history watchable:
- open loop: "There is one letter we have not read yet." Pay it off 1-3 minutes later.
- chapter-end cliffhanger: end every chapter on the question the next one answers.
- sudden reveal after slow build: three quiet sentences, then the fact that changes everything.
- human scale: after a big number, one named person.
