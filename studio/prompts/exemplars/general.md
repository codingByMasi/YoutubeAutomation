A coffee chain sells you a four-dollar latte. The milk costs twenty cents. So where does the rest go? Rent, mostly.
A corner shop in a busy city can cost more per month than the barista earns in a year. That is the first surprise.
The second is quieter. The chain is not really in the coffee business. It is in the real-estate business, and the
cup is the rent receipt. Hold that thought, because the third surprise is the one that decides who survives.
